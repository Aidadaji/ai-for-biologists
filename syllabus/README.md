## 📚 Course Description

This course teaches biologists, life-science students, and R&D professionals how to use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, NotebookLM) as research assistants **without compromising scientific rigor**.

Unlike generic "AI for X" courses, this one is:
- ✅ **Biology-specific** (gene name verification, pathway checks, citation fabrication)
- ✅ **India-aware** (DBT, CSIR, ICMR, UGC, INFLIBNET, Shodhganga)
- ✅ **Verification-first** (every AI claim is checked)
- ✅ **Hands-on** (20 labs with real datasets, not just theory)
- ✅ **Capstone-driven** (you leave with a finished deliverable)

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. **Find and verify** relevant research papers 3× faster than manual methods
2. **Design experiments** with AI assistance, including proper controls and statistical power
3. **Analyze data** with AI while catching errors before they corrupt your results
4. **Write manuscripts** that meet UGC/CSIR academic integrity standards
5. **Create presentations** and grant proposals that are compelling and accurate
6. **Apply ethical frameworks** for AI use in Indian biology research contexts

### Week 6+ — Capstone (Final 2 weeks)
**Time:** 15-25 hours

Choose ONE path:

#### Path A: Literature Review (15-20 hrs)
- 5-page literature review on a topic of your choice
- Include verification log showing you caught AI hallucinations
- Submit for peer review

#### Path B: Research Proposal (20-25 hrs)
- 10-page research proposal for a hypothetical project
- Includes hypothesis, methods, expected outcomes, timeline
- Submit for peer review

#### Path C: Manuscript Draft (20-25 hrs)
- Complete draft manuscript (Intro + Methods + Results + Discussion)
- Can be based on your own unpublished work
- Submit for peer review

**All paths require:**
- AI usage log (what you used AI for, how you verified)
- Verification checklist (every claim checked)
- Peer review of 2 other students' capstones

**Certificate awarded upon capstone approval.**
