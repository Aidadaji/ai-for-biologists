# Capstone Grading Rubric

**Pass/Fail grading** — but here's what we look for.

---

## 📊 Universal grading criteria (all 3 paths)

### 1. Verification Log (30%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **Excellent** | 8+ hallucinations caught with detailed verification method, time, and correction |
| **Good** | 5-7 hallucinations caught with verification |
| **Pass** | 5 hallucinations caught (minimum) |
| **Fail** | <5 hallucinations caught OR verification is hand-wavy |

**What we look for:**
- ✅ Each hallucination has: AI claim, verification method, correct info, time to catch, lesson learned
- ✅ Mix of hallucination types (DOI, gene name, statistic, etc.)
- ✅ Evidence you actually verified (not made up)

### 2. AI Usage Log (20%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **Excellent** | All 6 modules covered + time saved estimates + specific tools + specific outputs |
| **Good** | All 6 modules covered with reasonable detail |
| **Pass** | All 6 modules mentioned (even briefly) |
| **Fail** | Missing modules OR no specifics |

**What we look for:**
- ✅ Honest accounting of what you used AI for
- ✅ Per-module breakdown
- ✅ Time saved estimates (rough is fine)
- ✅ Specific tools + specific use cases

### 3. Writing Quality (20%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **Excellent** | Publication-ready prose; clear logical flow; no grammatical errors |
| **Good** | Minor revisions needed; clear meaning |
| **Pass** | Readable but needs polishing |
| **Fail** | Unclear, ungrammatical, or AI-generated without editing |

**What we look for:**
- ✅ Sounds like you wrote it (not AI)
- ✅ Logical structure
- ✅ Clear conclusions
- ✅ Appropriate tone for target venue

### 4. Citation Quality (15%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **Excellent** | Every citation verified + DOI links + 0% retraction rate + Indian + international balance |
| **Good** | All citations verified, minor gaps |
| **Pass** | Most citations verified, no fabricated DOIs |
| **Fail** | Fabricated DOIs found OR missing verification |

**What we look for:**
- ✅ All DOIs resolve
- ✅ All DOIs point to correct papers
- ✅ No retracted papers
- ✅ Reasonable balance (not all from one journal/year)

### 5. AI Disclosure (10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **Excellent** | Specific, transparent, follows UGC/CSIR guidelines |
| **Good** | Clear disclosure of AI use |
| **Pass** | Some disclosure present |
| **Fail** | No disclosure (academic misconduct) |

**Sample good disclosure:**
> "Claude (Anthropic, version 3.5) was used for language editing and reference formatting in this manuscript. All scientific content, data interpretation, and conclusions are the authors' own. The authors reviewed and edited all AI-generated text for accuracy."

### 6. Peer Review (5%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **Excellent** | Reviewed 2 capstones thoroughly, gave constructive feedback |
| **Good** | Reviewed 2 capstones |
| **Pass** | Reviewed at least 1 capstone |
| **Fail** | No peer review submitted |

## 📊 Path-specific criteria (70%)

### Path A: Literature Review

| Criteria | Weight | What we look for |
|----------|-------:|------------------|
| **Scope** | 15% | Clear, well-defined scope |
| **Themes** | 25% | Major themes identified and supported |
| **Contradictions** | 20% | Contradictions/gaps in literature addressed |
| **Synthesis** | 25% | Original synthesis (not just summary) |
| **Future directions** | 15% | Logical next steps identified |

### Path B: Research Proposal

| Criteria | Weight | What we look for |
|----------|-------:|------------------|
| **Specific Aims clarity** | 20% | Aims clearly stated, hypotheses testable |
| **Background** | 15% | Sufficient context, gap identified |
| **Preliminary Data** | 10% | Real or plausible preliminary data |
| **Methods** | 30% | Detailed enough to evaluate feasibility |
| **Timeline + Budget** | 15% | Realistic, justified |
| **Impact** | 10% | Clear significance |

### Path C: Manuscript Draft

| Criteria | Weight | What we look for |
|----------|-------:|------------------|
| **IMRaD structure** | 20% | Standard, complete sections |
| **Methods reproducibility** | 20% | Detailed enough to replicate |
| **Results clarity** | 25% | Logical order, figures referenced |
| **Discussion depth** | 25% | Compare to literature, acknowledge limitations |
| **Writing quality** | 10% | Publication-ready prose |

---

## ✅ Pass = 70% overall + all required components

You pass if:
- [x] 70%+ overall score
- [x] Verification log (5+ hallucinations)
- [x] AI usage log (all 6 modules)
- [x] No fabricated DOIs in final work
- [x] AI disclosure present
- [x] Peer review completed (2 capstones)

## 📤 What to submit

### One ZIP file containing:

```
your-name-capstone.zip
├── capstone.md  (or .pdf)
├── verification-log.md
├── ai-usage-log.md
├── references.bib  (or .docx)
├── peer-review-feedback.md  (your reviews of 2 others)
└── supplementary/  (any extra materials)
```

## 🕐 Review timeline

- Submission → review starts within 7 days
- Review completion → 14 days after submission
- Certificate issued → within 21 days of approval

If revisions needed, you'll get specific feedback and 7 days to revise.

---

## 🤝 Honest assessment

We grade **honestly but kindly**. The goal is for you to learn, not to fail.

Common reasons for revision request (not failure):
- Verification log needs more detail
- Some citations need re-verification
- AI usage log is too brief
- Writing needs more polish (we suggest specific edits)

Common reasons for actual failure:
- Fabricated DOIs not flagged in verification log
- Entire sections clearly AI-generated without editing
- No AI disclosure
- Plagiarism detected (Turnitin flags >30% AI)

We aim for **90%+ pass rate** on first submission.
