## 🎯 Who this course is for

| Audience | What you'll learn |
|----------|-------------------|
| **BSc/MSc/PhD students** | Cut literature review time by 70%; write better papers faster; ace your thesis defense |
| **R&D scientists** | Use AI as a research assistant without compromising scientific rigor |
| **Biotech professionals** | Draft grants, protocols, and reports 3× faster with proper verification |
| **Faculty / principal investigators** | Build AI-fluent labs; teach your students responsibly |

**Prerequisites:** Basic biology knowledge (any branch). No coding required for most modules. No prior AI experience needed.

## 📚 What you get

This is a **6-week self-paced course** with:

- 📖 **30+ video lessons** (8-10 hrs/week)
- 🧪 **20 hands-on labs** with real biological datasets
- 📜 **3 capstone paths** (literature review / research proposal / manuscript draft)
- 🎓 **Certificate of completion** (digital badge + PDF)
- 🇮🇳 **Indian bio context** throughout (DBT, CSIR, ICMR, UGC, NMC, DBT-FIST, SERB)
- ⚠️ **Verified examples** — every AI output in this course has been checked by a domain expert

## 🗂️ Course structure

### Module 0 — Onboarding & AI Fluency Foundations (Day 1)
The **AI Fluency 4Ds framework** + the **D.I.C.E. method** for prompts. Why this matters for biology specifically.

### Module 1 — AI for Literature Review (Week 1) ✅
Smart paper discovery · Paper summarization · Catching AI hallucinations · Building a literature matrix.

### Module 2 — AI for Experimental Design (Week 2)
Hypothesis generation · Controls and variables · Sample size & power · Reagent/protocol lookup.

### Module 3 — AI for Data Analysis (Week 3)
Spreadsheet intelligence · Statistics without tears · Bioinformatics assistance · Interpreting AI analysis.

### Module 4 — AI for Scientific Writing (Week 4)
Outlining · Methods automation · Results prose · Citations & references · Responding to reviewers.

### Module 5 — AI for Presentations & Communication (Week 5)
Slide decks · Conference abstracts · Grant proposals · Posters.

### Module 6 — AI Ethics & Responsible Use (Week 6)
What AI should NOT do · Academic integrity (UGC/CSIR rules) · Data privacy · Bias · When AI is wrong.

### 🏆 Final Capstone (End of Week 6)
Choose one: literature review, research proposal, or manuscript draft.

## 🇮🇳 Indian context — what makes this course different

Unlike generic "AI for X" courses, this one is **built for Indian biology researchers**:

| Section | India-specific content |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Module 0** | UGC guidelines on AI use in academia (2024 circular) |
| **Module 1** | DBT e-Library, NIPER, AIIMS databases; how to access paywalled papers legally via INFLIBNET |
| **Module 2** | IAEC (Institutional Animal Ethics Committee) protocol drafts; CPCSEA guidelines |
| **Module 3** | NCBI India mirror, Bt brinjal case study (regulatory data analysis) |
| **Module 4** | CSIR/UGC paper structure; addressing reviewer comments on Indian-context research |
| **Module 5** | SERB-DST grant proposal templates; DST INSPIRE fellowship applications |
| **Module 6** | ICMR AI ethics guidelines (2025 draft); data localization under DPDP Act 2023 |

## ⚠️ What this course is NOT

This course is **not** about:
- ❌ Building AI models or training neural networks (see AlterLab's deep learning skills instead)
- ❌ Replacing wet-lab work with AI
- ❌ Skipping peer review or domain expert verification
- ❌ Teaching AI to make clinical or safety-critical decisions

We teach biologists to **use AI as a power tool**, not as a replacement for scientific judgment.
