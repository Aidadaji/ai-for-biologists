The problem with AI in biology

🚨

AI hallucinates citations

5-20% of AI-generated biology citations don't exist or are wrong. Submit a paper with fabricated DOIs and you're flagged.

🧬

Wrong gene names

LLMs confidently invent gene symbols. Without verification, your primer design could be targeting a non-existent gene.

📊

Misinterpreted statistics

AI suggests the wrong statistical test, fabricates p-values, or "explains" results that don't match the paper's actual data.

🇮🇳

Western-centric training

Most AI tools miss Indian journals, theses (Shodhganga), and regulatory frameworks (DBT, ICMR, UGC).
