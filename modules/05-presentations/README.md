# Module 5 — AI for Presentations & Communication

**Duration:** Week 5 (~8-10 hours)
**Outcome:** Create compelling slides, abstracts, grant proposals, and posters 3× faster.

---

## Why this module matters

Conference deadlines + grant cycles = high-pressure writing. AI helps you:
- Generate slide structures fast
- Draft grant Specific Aims pages
- Format abstracts to word limits
- Critique your own posters

---

## 📖 Lessons

### Lesson 5.1 — Slide Deck Generation (60 min)
- The "10-20-30" rule (10 slides, 20 min, 30pt font)
- AI for slide structure, you for visuals
- **Lab 5.1:** Convert a paper to a 10-slide talk outline

### Lesson 5.2 — Conference Abstracts (45 min)
- Word-count discipline (200-300 words typical)
- Hook + Gap + Method + Finding + Impact template
- **India context:** SBC, IBS, Indian Science Congress abstract formats
- **Lab 5.2:** Draft 3 abstracts for 3 different conferences

### Lesson 5.3 — Grant Proposal Writing (90 min)
- **India-specific templates:**
  - SERB-DST Early Career Research Award
  - DST INSPIRE Faculty Fellowship
  - DBT Ramalingaswami Re-entry Fellowship
  - CSIR-EMR funding
  - ICMR Adhoc Research Project
- Specific Aims pages (most important 1 page)
- Budget justification narratives
- **Lab 5.3:** Draft a Specific Aims page for SERB-ECRA

### Lesson 5.4 — Posters That Don't Suck (45 min)
- The "5-second rule" (grasp it in 5 seconds)
- AI for layout suggestions, your judgment for hierarchy
- **Lab 5.4:** Critique a real poster (sample provided)

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

### Grant proposals
1. **Too ambitious** — 4 aims in 1 page → reviewers reject as unfocused
2. **Missing preliminary data** — Indian reviewers value this heavily
3. **No Indian context** — for DBT/SERB, show relevance to India
4. **Vague methodology** — "We will analyze..." → "We will use RT-qPCR with primers in Table 1..."
5. **Ignoring budget reality** — Don't ask for ₹10L for a consumables-heavy project

### Conference abstracts
1. **Too vague** — "We studied X" → no methods, no results
2. **No numbers** — "Significant increase" → "2.3-fold increase, p<0.01"
3. **Over-claiming** — "We proved" → "Our data suggest"
4. **Missing impact** — Always end with "why does this matter"

### Posters
1. **Too much text** — 500+ words → unreadable
2. **Tiny fonts** — <24pt for body, <32pt for headings
3. **Bad color contrast** — yellow on white, red on black
4. **No clear takeaway** — viewer should "get it" in 5 seconds
